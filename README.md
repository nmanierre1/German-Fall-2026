## Suggested packing list

**Pack for a week of city walking and a cold, potentially wet castle day. Quantities include what you wear on the flight.**

### Documents & money

- [ ] Passport; physical US driver's license and IDP for each driver
- [ ] Venture X, backup card and debit card; know your PIN
- [ ] Saved flight, rental, accommodation and ticket confirmations
- [ ] Glasses/contacts and any personal medication in your carry-on

### Clothing

- [ ] **1 warm coat**, ideally wind/rain resistant; wear it on the flight
- [ ] **2 warm midlayers** — sweaters or fleece
- [ ] **4–5 shirts** and **1 thermal base-layer set**
- [ ] **2 pairs of trousers**
- [ ] **8 pairs underwear** and **7–8 pairs socks**, with a few warm pairs
- [ ] Sleepwear; beanie, light gloves and scarf
- [ ] **1 pair of broken-in, water-resistant walking shoes with good grip** — wear these; no second pair needed unless you want one
- [ ] Compact umbrella or lightweight rain shell if your coat is not rainproof

### Electronics & toiletries

- [ ] Phone, charging cables and compact charger
- [ ] **European Type C/F plug adapter**; check each device/charger supports **230 V** before plugging it in
- [ ] Power bank **in carry-on**, not checked baggage; check the [airline rules](https://www.discover-airlines.com/us/en) and [FAA guidance](https://www.faa.gov/hazmat/packsafe/lithium-batteries)
- [ ] Earbuds/headphones; optional car phone mount and compatible charging cable
- [ ] Toothbrush, toothpaste, deodorant and other toiletries; check carry-on liquid limits

### Day bag

- [ ] Small backpack, reusable water bottle, tissues and lip balm
- [ ] Sunglasses and a small snack for driving/castle day

**Keep passports, cards, medication, phone and chargers with you during the flight.** Leave room for coffee beans and souvenirs.
